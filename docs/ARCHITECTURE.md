## Problem Statement

The firm (TCIA — trade credit and AR protection brokerage) maintains a dedicated **"Prospecting" pipeline** in HubSpot containing six kinds of high-intent prospects:
- **`re_engage_former_client`** — companies the firm has worked with before but who currently aren't placed with the firm.
- **`re_engage_bor_prospect`** — companies known to currently hold a policy with another broker (Broker-of-Record candidates).
- **`re_engage_lost_opportunity`** — companies the firm previously quoted but who didn't close at the time.
- **`coi_referral`** — Center-of-Influence referrals (accountant, attorney, peer broker).
- **`banker_abl_referral`** — referrals from asset-based-lending bankers or commercial bankers.
- **`cold_new_prospect`** — no prior TCIA relationship; sourced via cold outbound or list-build.

Today, no automated system surfaces these pipeline deals at the right moment for re-engagement. The user (an SDR with decision-maker authority) runs a manual 3–6 month general outreach cadence delivering 100-word, current-event-anchored, value-first emails with low/no-pressure CTAs followed by phone calls. The manual cadence works (one personally-attributed close from a reactivated lost-quote in the last 12 months) but is inconsistent and misses high-leverage moments — specifically, the 60-day window before a prospect's policy renewal, when buyers are actively shopping.

Build an internal automation that:
1. Replaces the manual 3–6 month general cadence with a scheduled queue (~2–3 emails per working day, evenly distributed across the Prospecting pipeline).
2. Adds a sharper trigger: 60 days before estimated renewal date (when known), surface the prospect with appropriate framing.
3. For each surfaced prospect, the local LLM produces **three artifacts** via the canonical prompt in `prompts/draft-pipeline.txt`:
   - **HubSpot Deal Note** — plain-text SDR brief: prospect type, known contacts, decision-maker gap, intel (news/leadership/industry signal, relationship anchor), recommended approach.
   - **Email Draft** — 2 subject options + 4-paragraph body (~150–200 words) in TCIA house voice with relationship-anchor logic, trade-credit-specific phrasing (*"shore up credit management," "ensure AR stays an asset," "leveraging every single carrier and AR resource"*), prospect-type-aware framing, and a soft CTA from the approved list.
   - **Cold Call Opening Script** — opening, context bridge, observation, open question, objection bridges.
4. Auto-creates a paired HubSpot phone-call task due 1–2 days after each email send, with the call script populated into `hs_task_body`.
5. Writes the deal note to HubSpot as a note engagement associated to the deal.
6. Enriches missing contacts via ZoomInfo (when deals lack associated contacts in HubSpot — common for older pipeline deals where the original buyer has moved on).
7. Lets the SDR review one card at a time (all 3 artifacts visible + editable) and click Send / Skip / Edit & Send.

The architecture must be implementation-ready and run inside the firm's existing self-hosted infrastructure (n8n, OpenClaw, local LLM server).

## Demand Evidence

- **Direct close**: User personally closed a reactivated lost-quote prospect within the last 12 months. The play converts in this firm's book of business.
- **Trigger hypothesis confirmed**: That close was driven by timing — the prospect's renewal cycle came back around. Calendar is the primary signal.
- **Existing manual practice**: 3–6 month general outreach cadence has been running for an unspecified period and is core to the firm's prospecting motion. The play has institutional history; this build accelerates and sharpens it.
- **Cost of inaction**: User estimates $150K+/yr in missed commission slipping through. Concrete back-of-envelope to be documented in v1 sponsor memo.

Gap: no historical conversion-rate baseline for the manual cadence. Success criteria (P10) measures incremental closes attributable to v1, not absolute conversion rate.

## Status Quo

- **Dedicated "Prospecting" pipeline in HubSpot** holds all three prospect types (BOR / previous-clients / previous-leads). Pipeline ID needs to be retrieved and stored in `.env`.
- Many pipeline deals lack associated contacts (original buyer moved on; never had a contact attached).
- Manual 3–6 month email + phone outreach. No systematic queue, no renewal-date-driven sharpening.
- HubSpot is the deal system of record. Apollo is a side-channel for sending. ZoomInfo is the firm's preferred contact enrichment tool.
- OneDrive holds policy history summaries (some — coverage varies).
- No `Closed Lost Reason` HubSpot property (mostly blank for these prospects).
- A `renewal_date` HubSpot custom property exists but is populated for a minority of prospects today.
- n8n, OpenClaw, and a local LLM server are already running on internal infrastructure for other workflows.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**User (v1)**: The SDR (project owner). Day-to-day workflow: open the queue UI in the morning, review 2–3 drafted emails, hit Send / Skip / Edit & Send. Phone-call tasks auto-appear in HubSpot for follow-up within 1–2 days.

**Wedge**: A scheduler with a drafting assistant bolted on. The outreach system already exists and works — what's broken is consistency and timing. v1 doesn't try to be creative; it ensures the right prospect surfaces at the right moment with a draft in the SDR's house voice and a paired phone task.

Not in v1: multi-user, permissions, team rollout, A/B testing, analytics dashboards, autonomous sending, the dead-folder sweep, OneDrive backfill of historical renewal dates, custom HubSpot properties beyond what already exists.

A Claude subagent received a structured cold-read briefing and returned the following independent observations (paraphrased for the doc; full transcript in session log):

- **Steelman**: A "personal lost-quote renewal radar" — not a product, a 1-person commission engine that turns dormant CRM rows into a 5-minute daily review queue. Ship metric = one extra bound policy per quarter, not feature velocity.
- **Load-bearing observation**: "The outreach system already exists and works. What's broken is consistency and timing, not content strategy." The build is a scheduler with a drafting assistant bolted on; this collapses ~80% of the design surface (no prompt engineering tournaments, no tone experiments, no A/B framework).
- **Premise challenge (P1)**: HubSpot `createdate` may drift from actual quote-sent date by weeks; policy terms aren't always 12 months. Original recommendation was to verify with a 20-deal forensic comparison. User chose to skip and instead build dual logic (round-robin fallback + renewal-date sharpening when available). Premise updated rather than verified.
- **Subagent prototype shape**: Validated the n8n + LLM + minimal review UI approach. Surfaced the Edit & Send diff log as a prompt-tuning corpus — cheap to add now, expensive to retrofit.

What both reads missed initially: the phone-call task pairing (P16, added during the conversation).

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: "Sheet + send" (minimal viable)
Single Python script on cron → LLM → Google Sheet. SDR copies/pastes manually, creates phone tasks by hand. Effort: S (~2 days). Rejected: manual phone-task creation will erode adoption after week 2; doesn't fit existing infra intent.

### Approach B: "Full local pipeline" (RECOMMENDED — locked)
n8n daily workflow + local LLM + minimal localhost review UI + auto phone-task creation + OpenClaw monitoring. Effort: M (~1–2 weeks). Selected: matches existing infrastructure, eliminates adoption friction, ships GitHub-ready architecture.

### Approach C: "Inbox-native drafts" (lateral)
Same backend, drafts land in email client Drafts folder via Gmail/Graph API. Effort: M (~1 week). Rejected: webhook reliability + Drafts-folder ergonomics risk after week 2.

### Module breakdown

#### Module 1: Scheduler (n8n workflow `sdr-scheduler`)

**Trigger**: Cron, daily at 06:00 local time.

**Query (HubSpot CRM Search API, batched, single call)**:

```
POST /crm/v3/objects/deals/search
{
  "filterGroups": [{"filters": [
    {"propertyName": "pipeline", "operator": "EQ",
     "value": "${HUBSPOT_PROSPECTING_PIPELINE_ID}"},
    {"propertyName": "notes_last_contacted_date", "operator": "LT",
     "value": <now - 90 days, epoch ms>}
  ]}],
  "properties": ["dealname","amount","createdate","closedate",
                 "renewal_date","hs_lastmodifieddate",
                 "${HUBSPOT_PROSPECT_TYPE_PROPERTY}",  // P20 — env-parameterized
                 "dealstage"],                          // also a candidate type-marker
  "associations": ["contacts","companies"],
  "limit": 200
}
```

Paginate with `after` cursor until exhausted. Stays under HubSpot's 100 req / 10s burst limit.

**Prospect-type classification (per deal)**: Extract `prospect_type` ∈ `{re_engage_former_client, re_engage_bor_prospect, re_engage_lost_opportunity, coi_referral, banker_abl_referral, cold_new_prospect}` from the HubSpot property named in `HUBSPOT_PROSPECT_TYPE_PROPERTY` env var (confirm property name and exact value strings in P20 → Open Questions). If property missing/null → **drop the deal**, log to `dropped` table with `reason = "missing_prospect_type"`. Do NOT default to a guessed type — wrong-type framing damages the relationship faster than no framing. User backfills the property; dropped deals re-enter eligibility on the next scheduler run.

**Contact selection rule**: For each deal, pick the contact in this priority order. The result also sets `contact_source` for the queue row:
1. Contact associated with `associationtype = primary` on the deal → set `contact_source = 'existing'`.
2. If no primary, first contact with `hs_email_optout = false` AND `hs_email_bad_address = false`, sorted by `createdate` desc → set `contact_source = 'existing'`.
3. If no contacts associated at all → tag item with `needs_enrichment = true` and **route to Module 2.5 (ZoomInfo enrichment)** AFTER Module 2's still-in-business check. Many Prospecting pipeline deals from years past have no associated contact because the original buyer moved on; the current CFO / credit manager is likely a new person. Enrichment is the path, not a drop.
4. If Module 2.5 returns a match → use the enriched contact (now created in HubSpot) → set `contact_source = 'zoominfo_enriched'`.
5. If enrichment fails → drop deal, log to `dropped` table with `reason = "no_valid_contact_after_enrichment"`.

**Flow ordering note**: Module 1 emits items tagged `needs_enrichment: bool`. Module 2 runs on all items. Module 2.5 runs only on Module 2 survivors with `needs_enrichment = true`. Module 3 runs on all survivors (enriched or not).

**Compliance filter (HARD, before enqueue)**: Drop any contact where `hs_email_optout = true`, `hs_email_bad_address = true`, or `hs_email_quarantined = true`. Never enqueue. This applies to both pre-existing AND ZoomInfo-enriched contacts (newly enriched contacts default to no opt-out, but if they later opt out, the cooldown applies on subsequent runs).

**Dedupe / cooldown check (join SQLite)**: For each candidate deal_id, skip if EITHER:
- Already exists in `queue` with `status IN ('pending','sent','edit-sent')` within last 30 days, OR
- Exists in `queue` with `status = 'skipped'` and `actioned_at > now - 30 days` (skip-cooldown), OR
- Exists in `dropped` and `dropped_at > now - 90 days`.

**Bucket classification**:
- **Sharp bucket** (if `renewal_date` populated): compute `target_send_date = renewal_date − 60 days`. If `target_send_date` falls in next 7 days → enqueue.
- **Round-robin bucket** (if `renewal_date` empty): assign deterministic year-long slot based on `int(md5(deal_id).hexdigest()[:8], 16) % WORKING_DAYS_PER_YEAR`. If today's working-day index matches → enqueue.

**Working-day index**: Computed from Jan 1 of current year, excluding weekends and a configurable US holiday list (`config/holidays.json`, hand-edited annually).

**Rate cap**: `DAILY_SEND_CAP = 3`. Sharp bucket has priority; if sharp items exceed cap, round-robin items *that overflowed for that day* are deferred to the next working day. Only overflow items defer — the year-long hash-based slot allocation itself is never re-hashed. Deferral is per-day overflow handling, not a reshuffle of the book. Small natural drift accepted in exchange for cap compliance.

**Day-1 bootstrap**: On first run, ingest all eligible deals from the Prospecting pipeline, classify by `prospect_type`, and enqueue only those whose slot matches today's working-day index (or whose sharp renewal window is open). The deterministic hash ensures stable allocation across reboots.

**Output**: list of `{deal_id, contact_id, company_id, send_reason: "renewal" | "round-robin"}` passed to Module 2.

#### Module 2: Still-in-business filter (n8n node + Apollo)

For each enqueued item:
1. Call Apollo `organizations/enrich` by company domain.
2. If response indicates inactive (`organization.exists = false`, website returns 404 twice, or no result) → drop, log to `dropped` SQLite table.
3. Optional secondary check: HTTP HEAD on company website. Two failures = drop.

Apollo's role here is narrow: company existence verification (cheap, fast). Contact-level enrichment is ZoomInfo's job (Module 2.5).

Pass survivors to Module 2.5 (if contact missing) or directly to Module 3 (if contact already valid).

#### Module 2.5: Contact enrichment (n8n node + ZoomInfo) — NEW

**Triggered for**: deals that survived Module 2 but lack any associated contact (per Module 1's contact selection rule, step 3).

**Pre-check (caching)**: Query SQLite `enrichment_cache` table for this `company_id`. If a successful enrichment exists within 90 days → reuse the contact. If a failed enrichment exists within 90 days → skip immediately (don't repeatedly query ZoomInfo for unfindable companies). Otherwise proceed.

**ZoomInfo query** — call `get_recommended_contacts` (preferred — built for "give me the right person at this company") or fall back to `search_contacts` with explicit filters:

```
search criteria:
  company_domain: <from HubSpot company record>
  OR company_name: <fallback if domain enrichment fails>
  job_titles_any_of: configurable list, default:
    - "Chief Financial Officer"
    - "CFO"
    - "Credit Manager"
    - "Director of Finance"
    - "VP Finance"
    - "Controller"
    - "VP, Risk Management"
  active_only: true
  return_email: true
  limit: 3
```

**Pick logic**: Highest match-confidence result with a valid email AND a title in the target list. Tie-break: prefer CFO > VP Finance > Director of Finance > Controller > Credit Manager > Risk Management.

**HubSpot writes (atomic — succeed all or rollback)**:
1. Create contact: `POST /crm/v3/objects/contacts` with `email`, `firstname`, `lastname`, `jobtitle`, custom property `enrichment_source = "ZoomInfo {YYYY-MM-DD}"`, `enrichment_confidence = <ZoomInfo confidence score>`.
2. Associate contact ↔ company: `PUT /crm/v3/objects/contacts/{contactId}/associations/companies/{companyId}/contact_to_company`.
3. Associate contact ↔ deal: `PUT /crm/v3/objects/contacts/{contactId}/associations/deals/{dealId}/contact_to_deal`.
4. Write to `enrichment_cache` table: `{company_id, contact_id, source: "zoominfo", confidence, enriched_at}`.

If any step fails, delete the contact (if created) and write a failed-attempt row to `enrichment_cache` with 90-day TTL. Log to OpenClaw.

**On successful enrichment**, set `contact_source = 'zoominfo_enriched'` on the in-flight item. This propagates to the queue row (Module 5) and into the LLM prompt (Module 4) so the email tone shifts slightly more formal.

**Edge case**: if the company itself is not in HubSpot (rare for Prospecting pipeline deals since the deal IS associated to a company, but possible for force-enqueued deals), Module 2.5 cannot create an association. Log to `dropped` with `reason = "company_not_in_hubspot"`. Company creation is out of scope for v1; user creates the company record manually if they want this deal in.

**Cost discipline**: ZoomInfo credits are real money. Module 1 should batch all enrichment-needing deals, then Module 2.5 fires once per scheduler run with a hard cap (`MAX_ENRICHMENTS_PER_DAY = 10`, configurable). If more than 10 deals need enrichment in a day, defer the excess to the next run (with cache writes preventing re-query waste).

Pass enriched survivors to Module 3.

#### Module 3: Enrichment (n8n nodes)

For each survivor, gather:

- **HubSpot deal context**: `amount` (→ quote_amount), `dealname` or custom product property (→ product_line), `createdate` (→ quote_date, formatted as "March 2024" human-readable). Plus last 3 engagements: GET `/crm/v3/objects/deals/{id}/associations/engagements`, sort by `createdate desc`, take first 3, fetch each engagement body via `/crm/v3/objects/engagements/{id}`, truncate body to 200 chars each.

- **Relationship anchor extraction (NEW)**: Search deal notes + engagement bodies for:
  1. Named past TCIA broker (cross-reference against an internal `config/tcia-brokers.json` roster — e.g. "Tom O'Connell"). First hit wins.
  2. Named internal agency contact (account exec, prior TCIA owner) from `hubspot_owner_id` history on the deal.
  3. Prior TCIA work history with the company (any past quote, policy, recorded touchpoint flagged in notes).
  4. If none → `relationship_anchor = null`. LLM is instructed (per `prompts/draft-pipeline.txt`) to drop the relationship paragraph entirely rather than manufacture one.
  Return as: `{anchor_type: "broker"|"agency_contact"|"prior_work"|null, anchor_name: string|null, anchor_context: string|null}`.

- **OneDrive policy excerpt**: Microsoft Graph search via `/search/query` (KQL) scoped to the policy folder driveId. Returns file metadata only. For first match, download via `/drives/{driveId}/items/{itemId}/content`. For .docx, extract text via `python-docx`; for .pdf, via `pypdf2`; for .txt, read directly. Truncate extracted text to 500 chars. Skip enrichment field if no match (LLM tolerates missing input).

- **Current event hook (TIGHTENED — trade-credit-specific signals only)**: Try in order, stop at first success:
  1. **M&A signal** (90-day window): ZoomInfo `enrich_scoops` (M&A scoop type) or PitchBook free-tier search → company appears in acquisition / divestiture / funding announcement within 90 days. Return event type + counterparty + date.
  2. **Leadership change signal** (90-day window): new CFO, Controller, VP Finance, or Treasurer hire at the company. Source: ZoomInfo `enrich_contacts` filtered by job-start-date < 90 days OR LinkedIn company-page scrape for "promoted to" / "new" / "joins as CFO" / "named Controller".
  3. **Sector financial stress signal**: industry-level (NOT company-level) indicator within 60 days — e.g., rising default rates in the company's NAICS, sector-wide carrier rate hardening, notable peer bankruptcy. Source: a curated `config/sector-signals.json` updated weekly by Daniel, OR an industry-news RSS filtered by sector.
  4. **Fallback `hook_source: "none"`** — no qualifying signal found. Set `hook_text = null`. The prompt's relationship-anchor logic takes over; if neither hook nor anchor resolves, the LLM falls back to a generic value-touch about TCIA's broader carrier panel.

  Generic news search (Google/Bing for any headline) is explicitly OFF the table — generic news rarely connects to credit exposure and dilutes the targeting.

Pass enriched bundles to Module 4 with the full payload: `{deal_id, company_id, contact_id, contact_email, contact_first_name, contact_last_name, company_name, company_acronym (if discoverable from notes), quote_amount, product_line, quote_date_human, renewal_date_or_null, send_reason, prospect_type, policy_excerpt_or_null, hook_text, hook_source, hook_metadata, relationship_anchor (object with anchor_type/anchor_name/anchor_context, or null), contact_source}`.

#### Module 4: LLM drafting (HTTP POST to local LLM endpoint)

**Endpoint**: configurable, default `http://localhost:11434/v1/chat/completions` (Ollama-style) or whatever the firm's local LLM exposes. Pass `response_format: {"type": "json_object"}` if the endpoint supports it; otherwise parse with a fallback regex-extraction step.

**Model floor**: Llama 3.1 8B is the minimum but unreliable for strict format adherence across 3 artifacts. Recommended: 70B class (Llama 3.1 70B, Mistral Large, Qwen 2.5 72B) or equivalent. Document chosen model in repo README.

**Prompt source**: Single canonical file at `prompts/draft-pipeline.txt` (path in `PROMPT_FILE_PATH` env var). The doc does NOT duplicate the prompt; the file is the source of truth for voice rules, TCIA-specific phrasing, the relationship-anchor fallback hierarchy, prospect-type framing, brand calibrations (Allianz/Euler, company acronyms), and negative constraints (NEVER use "navigate"). Module 4 loads the file at draft time and substitutes the per-deal inputs.

**Single LLM call, three artifacts**. The prompt instructs the model to return one JSON object with all three deliverables:

```json
{
```

#### Module 6: Review UI (localhost web app)

**Tech**: Simple Express + plain HTML/JS, or a single-file Python `bottle` app. ~200 lines total. Served on `localhost:5679/queue` — **n8n already owns 5678 by default**, so use a different port. Default `REVIEW_UI_PORT=5679` (configurable).

**Routes**:
- `GET /queue` — render next pending row as a card (oldest `enqueued_at` first, sharp bucket prioritized). If queue empty, show "all caught up."
- `POST /queue/{id}/send` — call Module 7 (HubSpot writes) with original draft body.
- `POST /queue/{id}/skip` — mark `status='skipped'`, set `actioned_at = now`. Cooldown enforced by Module 1 (30 days).
- `POST /queue/{id}/edit-send` — accept edited body, store `edit_diff = {"before": draft_body, "after": edited_body}` as JSON, call Module 7 with edited body.
- `POST /queue/force` — manual injection. Accepts `{deal_id}`, validates deal exists in HubSpot, bypasses dedupe and rate cap, runs Modules 2–5 inline, returns the new queue row ID. **Compliance filter is NOT bypassed** — opt-out / bounced / quarantined contacts are still dropped (this is a legal hard requirement, separate from operational dedupe/cap). SDR-only escape hatch for "push this specific lead through right now."

**LLM-failure cards**: When `draft_body` starts with `[LLM format failure...]`, the card UI shows a prominent banner: "AUTO-DRAFT FAILED — please hand-write." The editable textarea is empty; subject is empty. Send / Skip still work.

**Card layout** (one prospect at a time, three-section panel):

**Header row**:
- Company name, contact name + email
- Original quote: amount + product + date
- **Prospect-type badge** (6 colors): "FORMER CLIENT" / "BOR PROSPECT" / "LOST OPPORTUNITY" / "COI REFERRAL" / "BANKER/ABL REFERRAL" / "COLD PROSPECT"
- **Contact-source badge**: "EXISTING CONTACT" or "ZOOMINFO-ENRICHED"
- **Send reason badge**: "RENEWAL (60 days out)" or "ROUND-ROBIN"
- **Anchor badge**: "BROKER: Tom O'Connell" / "PRIOR WORK" / "NO ANCHOR" (helps SDR verify framing)
- Renewal date (if known) + hook source/text inline

**Section 1 — Deal Note** (top, read-mostly):
- Editable textarea pre-filled with `deal_note_body`
- Toggle: "Edit before posting to HubSpot" (defaults off; SDR usually just reviews)

**Section 2 — Email Draft** (middle, primary focus):
- Subject line: dropdown of 2 options (defaults to subject_options[0]) + freeform edit
- Editable textarea pre-filled with `draft_body`
- Live word count display (warns red if outside 150–200)

**Section 3 — Call Script** (bottom, collapsed by default):
- Click to expand
- Each section editable: Opening, Context Bridge, Observation, Open Question, Objection Bridges (3 sub-fields)

**Buttons (apply to whole card)**:
- **Send** — writes all three to HubSpot as configured in Module 7. Uses original artifacts.
- **Skip** — resurface in 30 days; no writes.
- **Edit & Send** — uses whatever the SDR edited; diff log captures the deltas for prompt tuning.

#### Module 7: HubSpot writes (n8n workflow `sdr-send`)

Triggered by Review UI on Send / Edit & Send. Writes **three artifacts** to HubSpot.

**Actions (in order; each step's success required before next)**:

1. **Email engagement**: `POST /crm/v3/objects/emails` with `hs_email_subject` (chosen subject from UI), `hs_email_text` (or HTML body), `hs_email_direction = "EMAIL"`, `hs_timestamp = <send time, epoch milliseconds>`. Associate to contact and deal via `associations` array. Sender identity: the user's HubSpot user ID (`hubspot_owner_id`) so the email appears as sent on behalf of the SDR, not a system account.

2. **Deal note** (SDR brief): `POST /crm/v3/objects/notes` with `hs_note_body = <deal_note_body>`, `hs_timestamp = <now, epoch ms>`. Associate to deal via `associations` array. This becomes the internal research record SDR or teammates can reference later.

3. **Phone-call task** with embedded call script: `POST /crm/v3/objects/tasks` with `hs_task_subject = "Follow up on reactivation email to {company}"`, `hs_task_body = <formatted call script>` (see formatting below), `hs_task_priority = "HIGH"`, `hs_task_status = "NOT_STARTED"`, `hs_task_type = "CALL"`, `hs_timestamp = <(now + 36 hours), epoch milliseconds>`. Associate to deal + contact + owner.

**Call script formatting (Markdown into `hs_task_body`)**:
```
## Cold Call Script — {company_name}

**OPENING**: {call_script.opening}

**CONTEXT BRIDGE**: {call_script.context_bridge}

**OBSERVATION**: {call_script.observation}

**OPEN QUESTION**: {call_script.open_question}

---

**OBJECTION BRIDGES**:
- *Already covered*: {call_script.objection_bridges.already_covered}
- *Not interested*: {call_script.objection_bridges.not_interested}
- *Send info*: {call_script.objection_bridges.send_info}

---

Email sent {sent_date} — subject: {chosen_subject}
```

4. Update SQLite row: `status = 'sent' | 'edit-sent'`, `actioned_at = now`, `hubspot_engagement_id`, `hubspot_note_id`, `hubspot_task_id`.

**Error recovery**: If step 1 succeeds but step 2 or 3 fails (network / 5xx), retry the failing step up to 3 times with exponential backoff. If still failing, save any successful HubSpot IDs to SQLite, leave failed ones as NULL, set `status = 'sent'` (email went out — partial success). Log to OpenClaw alert. Module 8's nightly sweep retries NULL note/task IDs the next day.

#### Module 8: OpenClaw monitoring

Watches the n8n workflows (`sdr-scheduler` and `sdr-send`) for:
- Execution failures (LLM down, HubSpot 429, Apollo 401, etc.)
- Queue depth anomalies (zero items enqueued for >2 days; >10 items enqueued in one day)
- Weekly conversion KPI digest (computed via SQL on the queue DB):

```sql
-- Sent count last 7 days
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM queue
  WHERE status IN ('sent','edit-sent') AND actioned_at > date('now','-7 days');

-- Skip rate
SELECT (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM queue WHERE status='skipped' AND actioned_at > date('now','-7 days')) * 1.0
     / (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM queue WHERE status IN ('sent','edit-sent','skipped') AND actioned_at > date('now','-7 days'));

-- Edit ratio
SELECT (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM queue WHERE status='edit-sent' AND actioned_at > date('now','-7 days')) * 1.0
     / (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM queue WHERE status IN ('sent','edit-sent') AND actioned_at > date('now','-7 days'));

-- Dropped count
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM dropped WHERE dropped_at > date('now','-7 days');

-- Per-type touch volume (sanity-check round-robin proportionality)
SELECT prospect_type, COUNT(*) FROM queue
  WHERE status IN ('sent','edit-sent') AND actioned_at > date('now','-7 days')
  GROUP BY prospect_type;

-- Per-contact-source breakdown (existing vs zoominfo_enriched conversion)
SELECT contact_source, status, COUNT(*) FROM queue
  WHERE actioned_at > date('now','-30 days')
  GROUP BY contact_source, status;

-- Enrichment success rate
SELECT
  CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN status='success' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS REAL) / COUNT(*)
FROM enrichment_cache
  WHERE enriched_at > date('now','-30 days');
```

**Phone-task completion read-back** (nightly): query HubSpot tasks via Search API with `hs_task_subject` operator `CONTAINS_TOKEN` value `"reactivation"` (HubSpot Search does not support `LIKE`); for each match, check `hs_task_status`. Cross-reference `hubspot_task_id` from SQLite to scope to tool-created tasks only. Compute completion rate, surface in weekly digest.

**Failed-artifact retry sweep** (nightly): query SQLite for rows where `status IN ('sent','edit-sent')` AND (`hubspot_task_id IS NULL` OR `hubspot_note_id IS NULL`). For each, retry the missing write (task creation or note creation). Backstop for Module 7 partial-success cases.

OpenClaw is the existing monitoring layer — this design adds two new workflow IDs to its watchlist and one nightly cron query; no new OpenClaw infrastructure.

## Open Questions

1. **5 approved CTAs**: needs authoring by the user before v1 launch. Suggested categories: comparison offer, one-page summary, market update, renewal options, no-pressure check-in. Stored in `prompts/ctas.json`.
2. **Round-robin slot allocation**: **RESOLVED**. Use deterministic hash (`md5(deal_id) % WORKING_DAYS_PER_YEAR`). Do NOT re-shuffle — re-shuffling breaks year-long pacing guarantees. If a deal becomes invalid (no valid contact, opted out), it's dropped; remaining deals keep their slots.
3. **OneDrive permissions**: confirm `Files.Read.All` Graph permission + admin consent. `Sites.Read.All` is SharePoint-scoped and won't reach personal OneDrive. App-only access to a user's personal OneDrive may require additional tenant configuration.
4. **LLM model**: confirm which model is loaded on the local server. Spec assumes 70B-class for word-count reliability. If only 8B available, retry-on-fail logic (Module 4) catches most failures but expect 20–30% manual-draft cards.
5. **News source selection**: spec offers Bing News API (1000 free calls/month) or NewsAPI (100 free calls/day). Pick one before launch and populate `NEWS_SOURCE` env var. NewsAPI free tier likely insufficient at 2–3 sends/day × enrichment lookups.
6. **Sponsor memo**: one-page memo documenting the $150K+ methodology — recommended for future-self reference and team rollout pitch.
7. **HubSpot sender identity**: emails sent via engagement API with `hubspot_owner_id` set to user's HubSpot user ID — verify this renders as "sent on behalf of" rather than from a system address in the prospect's inbox. Test before launch with internal email.
8. **Legal basis for ZoomInfo-enriched outreach**: cold contacts sourced from ZoomInfo have no prior relationship or opt-in with the firm. CAN-SPAM permits B2B cold outreach with a valid physical address and one-click unsubscribe; CCPA/GDPR may require documented legitimate-interest basis. Confirm firm's outbound policy supports this before launch. The email template footer must include the firm's physical address and an unsubscribe path (HubSpot handles this automatically when sent via engagement API).
9. **ZoomInfo persona list calibration**: the default target titles (CFO, Credit Manager, Director of Finance, VP Finance, Controller, VP Risk Management) are insurance-broker assumptions. After 30 days, audit which titles actually convert and tighten the list. Bad titles waste ZoomInfo credits.
10. **ZoomInfo plan tier & cost ceiling**: at 10 enrichments/day × 250 working days = 2,500 lookups/year. Confirm firm's ZoomInfo plan covers this with headroom for the bootstrap surge (Day 1 may try to enrich a large backlog at once — set `MAX_ENRICHMENTS_PER_DAY` low for first week).
11. **Prospect-type property identifier (P20)**: confirm the exact HubSpot property name and value enumeration that distinguishes BOR / previous-client / previous-lead within the Prospecting pipeline. Candidates: a custom enum property (e.g. `prospect_type`), a tag/list, or the deal stage within the pipeline (each stage = one type). The query in Module 1 and the schema's `prospect_type` column depend on this. Without it, all deals default to `previous_lead` framing — usable but suboptimal.
12. **Per-type conversion baselines unknown**: are BOR prospects, previous clients, and previous leads roughly equally reactivatable, or are some categories meaningfully easier? After 90 days of v1 data, segment the success metric by `prospect_type` to inform whether the cadence rules or persona targeting should differ per type in v1.1.

## Success Criteria

**Primary (kill criterion)**: ≥1 attributable reactivated close inside 90 days of v1 running. If zero, halt and reassess premises (especially P1 trigger validity).

**Secondary (health signals)**:
- Weekly: ≥2 emails sent per working day average (rate cap holds).
- Edit ratio < 40% by week 4 (prompt is good enough; SDR isn't constantly rewriting).
- Skip rate < 25% by week 4 (still-in-business filter is working; queue isn't full of dead companies).
- Phone-task completion rate > 70% (tasks are being acted on, not abandoned).
- **ZoomInfo enrichment success rate** > 60% by week 4 (when triggered, lookup finds a usable contact more than half the time).
- **Enrichment-then-outreach conversion** (tracked via `contact_source` column): compared to existing-contact outreach. Important for justifying ZoomInfo spend.
- ZoomInfo lookups per day ≤ `MAX_ENRICHMENTS_PER_DAY` cap (cost discipline holds).
- **Per-type touch volume** roughly proportional to pipeline composition (e.g., if pipeline is 40/40/20 BOR/prev-client/prev-lead, weekly touches should land near same ratio — sanity check that round-robin isn't skewing).
- **Per-type reply / engagement rate** logged for v1.1 calibration (do BOR prospects respond differently than previous-leads? Inform whether to split cadence rules per type).

**Sponsor metric** (self-funded but useful for future scaling pitch): commission attributable to tool / SDR hours saved per week.
